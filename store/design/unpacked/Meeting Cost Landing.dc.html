Privacy

Rates never leave your computer.

Meeting Cost has no server, no account and no analytics. It reads only the event you have open, does the arithmetic inside the page, and sends nothing anywhere.

  • ✓Rates are stored on this computer only, never synced to a browser account.
  • ✓Runs only on calendar.google.com and Outlook on the web. No other site.
  • ✓Nothing read from the page is stored or transmitted. The extension makes no network requests at all.
  • ✓Rates are deleted when you reset the options or remove the extension. Open source under the MIT license.
